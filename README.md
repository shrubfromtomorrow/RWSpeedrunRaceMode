# Rain World Speedrun Race Mode

## Details

This mod prevents the player from dying to all sources of death and ideally respawns them back at their last room entry shortcut.
Select a starting shelter and ending room in the slugcat select menu with race mode turned on. If race mode is off, death causes are still prevented but other race mode aspects will not be present.

Empty or unknown starting rooms will cause the mod to start the campaign as usual. Known Watcher rooms will spawn watcher with ripple as though the intro had been completed.

Time ends when the camera transitions to the ending room.

Death count and ending room are shown on the map when held.

Dying of hypothermia, when the region is doing deathrain and the room is flooding, electrified to the point of being able to kill the player, or the player is being pushed by rain, sandstorm has progressed to the point of being able to kill the player, in LC_FINAL as Artificer, or holding the mercy keybind will result in dying properly.

If the cause of death is a bomb, you will lose your grasps.
Grasps are saved when the player is sent to shortcut, spasmers (jellyfish) and poisontrackers (tardigrades) are removed, many other state variables are reset, not all.

If the player somehow does not have a defined entry shortcut, they will default to teleporting to what would be their karma flower position if they have it, or the middle of the first screen of the room if they do not.
